03 / The accounting promiseThree balances. Different permissions.

A deposit is not income.
A repair reserve stays with its item.

Operator income
80%

Of the rental fee in the demo configuration. Withdrawable by the operator.

Item repair reserve
20%

Of the rental fee in the demo configuration. Spent only on approved repairs.

Refundable deposit
Separate

Held as a rental liability. Returned in full or reduced by an approved claim.

Illustrative split, not a completed live payment. Human assessment still determines damage; unresolved votes can delay settlement.

Keep value exact

TransactionStorage

Commit the final photo bytes
